# Edits: how the shown text differs from the raw interview

This page lists the kinds of edit made to Bex's interview before it was voiced, with counts. It never quotes the words that were hidden, and the raw interview is not published. Everything here is read from the written record of the edits.

## Exchanges left out

Four statements were left out, each with the answer to it (8 lines in all). They sit too close to the subject of minors for this show, so the same four are left out of every episode.
The statements keep their numbers from the test, so the gaps show in the transcript.

## Hidden words

38 words were hidden. Each is shown in the transcript as a row of solid blocks, one block for each letter, exactly as the video shows it. In the video the voice says "ahem" where a hidden word falls, with a short pause before it and after it, so the line carries on like a held-back sneeze.

- The words were chosen by Beth: a short list of plainly explicit words, matched as whole words in any capital letters. They fall into 5 groups, and the groups account for 33, 2, 1, 1 and 1 of the 38.
- 35 of them are in Beth's lines (the test's statements), spread over 31 of the 61 questions. 3 are in Bex's answers.
- The voice reads the text exactly as it is shown here.
- The words themselves are not listed, on purpose.

## Text taken out of answers

In 3 of Bex's 61 answers, the rule "emphasis marks (the words stay)" matched 6 times, and each match was taken out.

These are small clean-ups, so the text reads cleanly. The words around them were left as they were.

## A wording change

The test writes "partner(s)". In 6 of Beth's questions it is shown, and spoken, as "partners" instead, because the first form does not read aloud well. Nothing else in those lines changed.

## Tags and the rating request

A small bracketed tag that the interview list puts on some statements was removed wherever it appeared.

Stray angle-bracket markers (start and end markers) that came through in the text were removed wherever they appeared.

The interview added a request to rate each statement from 1 to 7 ("How much do you agree with this statement, from 1 to 7, 7 being most strongly agree?"). It is kept in full on statement 1 and left off the other 59 statements, so the video does not repeat it 60 times.

## Automatic greetings

An AI companion's automatic first greeting is always removed from an episode. In this interview there was none to remove.

## Typography

So that every file here is plain text, transcript.md writes the shown text's typography in plain ASCII: 119 curly quotation marks or apostrophes written as straight ones, and 1 long dash written as two hyphens ("--"). Nothing else about the words changed. transcript.jsonl keeps the exact characters, written as JSON escapes.

## Capital letters

In 1 line the video shows a word in capital letters where the interview has it in lower case. The text here is as the video shows it.

## Written for the show

Beth's intro and sign-off (2 lines) are not part of the interview. They are written for the show, and the transcript marks them as the intro and the sign-off.

## Nothing else

Nothing else was changed. The exchanges keep the order of the interview, and no words were added to Bex's answers or reordered in them.

## Notes from the maker

Bex sometimes put asterisks round a word for emphasis. The asterisks were taken out and the words stay. Two stray start and end markers that came through in one answer were taken out too.

Beth added two more words to the hidden list for Bex's interview, as a precaution, after her answers had been read. Three of the hidden words are in Bex's own answers, and each is spoken as "ahem" like the others.

For this episode the intro and the sign-off were drafted by an AI session at Beth's request, after the ones she wrote for episode 1, and Beth approved them as drafted before they were voiced.
